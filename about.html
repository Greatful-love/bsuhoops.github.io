Who We Are

Cardinal Basketball
— Student Run

Ball State Men's Basketball Club is a student-operated competitive basketball program affiliated with Ball State University's Recreation Services and The NCBBA (The National Club Basketball Association).

We compete against other club programs in our confernce, oustide our conference, and compete in matches against local NAIA teams throughout the semester. Our goal is to give competitive basketball players the chance to keep playing high level organized basketball after high school — on their terms, at their school.

No matter your background — high school varsity, intramural, or just pick-up ball — if you love the game and want to compete, we want to hear from you, roster spots are limited though.

How to Join

  1. 1
    Fill out the interest form

    Submit your name, year, and position so we have your info before tryouts.

  2. 2
    Come to tryouts

    Dates are announced each semester on our social media and news page. Show up ready to compete.

  3. 3
    Pay club dues & register with RecServices

    Once you make the roster, dues cover your jersey, tournament fees, and facility access.

  4. 4
    Show up and hoop

    Practice twice a week, compete in tournaments, and be part of the Cardinal basketball family.
